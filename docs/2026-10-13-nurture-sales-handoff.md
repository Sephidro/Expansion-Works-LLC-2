# Nurture sales-page handoff

Date: October 13, 2026. Source pack v2.0 plus Xavier's latest direction.

Xavier confirmed career experience across multiple charter schools, consultants, playwrights, and cosmetics companies. The page records that experience without inventing client outcomes. The 207/105 school counts retain their exact meaning.

Positioning now emphasizes done-for-you acknowledgment, nurture, and follow-up before increasing ad spend. Missed-call texts and an honest review request are selectable elements of one agreed journey, not unlimited channels or sequences. Current scope remains one service/journey, up to two systems/two connections, one acknowledgment and up to four follow-up messages, including a review message where agreed. Phone routing, sender eligibility, consent, registration, fees, status handling and review trigger must be verified in setup confirmation. Reviews are invited consistently without positive-only screening or incentives. No automated prospect conversations or client sales calls are supplied.

Standalone sales-page eyebrows were removed rather than hidden for search engines. The plan uses three rounded, reader-controlled cards with short HTML/CSS motion, buttons, arrow/Home/End keyboard navigation, reduced-motion behavior and readable no-JavaScript fallback. It does not auto-advance while a buyer reads. Context and evidence notes are at the bottom; package limits remain visible near the offer.
