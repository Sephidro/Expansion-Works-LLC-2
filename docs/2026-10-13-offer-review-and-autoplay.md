Implemented: plan cards progress every ten seconds while visible. Pause/Play remains available. Hover suspends progression; keyboard focus stops it until explicit Play. Background tabs and offscreen cards suspend progression. Reduced-motion users start paused. Manual navigation, keyboard tabs and no-JavaScript full-card fallback remain.

Research correction: established adoption and vendor revenues do not answer whether follow-up increases buyer revenues. Randomized email experiments support revenue gains in specific settings; broad benchmarks show efficient attribution, not a clean all-business causal estimate. The claim “relevant, well-run follow-up can improve sales” is supported directionally. No representative estimate comparing all businesses with established nurture against all businesses without it was verified. Do not equate attributed revenue with incremental lift.

Primary sources checked October 13:
- Sahni, Zou and Chintagunta, Management Science, 70 randomized email-discount experiments at one ticket resale platform: average spend increased $3.03 (37.2%) during promotion windows; most gain was not discount redemption.
