Your data at a glance.

Progress stays in your browser. Submitted inquiries go to Expansion Works.

What stays in your browser

Local storage holds your answers, progress, result, random visitor ID, attribution, and recent events so you can return.

Your quiz answers are sent only when you submit a form with the brief attached.

What a form sends

Formspree sends your submitted fields and attached brief to Expansion Works: contact details, service, process, and any optional project information.

We use it to handle your request, assess fit, and keep business records. We do not sell it or automatically subscribe you to marketing.

Measurement and hosting

The site prepares interaction events for analytics. Durable collection has not been verified. Hosting and form providers process standard request data needed to deliver their services.

Google Fonts is loaded from Google. External product links send you to the named vendor, whose own privacy terms then apply.

Referral links and recommendations

Recommendations may favor a partner, a non-partner, your current tool, or no purchase. Compensated links are labeled. Qualifying purchases may earn us a commission at no added cost to you.

Your choices

  • Ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted by emailing xavier.brandmanager@gmail.com.

Requests will be handled subject to any records Expansion Works must keep for legal, security, or legitimate business reasons.
