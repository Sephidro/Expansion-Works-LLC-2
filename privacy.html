PLAIN-LANGUAGE POLICY // UPDATED AUGUST 15, 2026

Privacy without the fog.

StackBrief collects only what is needed to keep the decision tool working, understand how people found it, and respond when someone deliberately sends a form.

What stays in your browser

Quiz answers, progress, the completed brief, a random visitor ID, referral and campaign details, and recent interaction events are stored in your browser using local storage. This lets you leave and return without starting over.

Those answers are not sent to Expansion Works just because you use the quiz. They leave the browser only when you deliberately submit a beta-review or implementation form that includes the brief.

What a form sends

When you submit a form, the fields you complete and any attached StackBrief context are sent through Formspree to Expansion Works. This can include your name, work email, website, revenue range, stated bottleneck, and the decisions in your brief.

That information is used to answer your request, evaluate whether a paid implementation makes economic sense, and maintain ordinary business records. It is not sold and a form submission does not add you to a generic marketing newsletter.

Measurement and hosting

The site prepares first-party events for tools such as Vercel Analytics, Plausible, or PostHog, but this repository does not currently configure an outside analytics destination. The hosting and form providers may process standard request information needed to deliver their services.

Google Fonts is loaded from Google. External product links send you to the named vendor, whose own privacy terms then apply.

Referral links and recommendations

A recommendation can point to a paid partner, a product that pays nothing, a tool you already own, or no purchase. Compensated links are labeled beside the recommendation. Expansion Works may earn a commission from a qualifying purchase through a labeled referral link, at no added cost to you.

Your choices

  • Clear this site’s local storage in your browser to remove saved quiz progress and local events.
  • Do not submit a form if you do not want the information sent to Expansion Works.
  • Ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted by emailing xavier.brandmanager@gmail.com.

Requests will be handled subject to any records Expansion Works must keep for legal, security, or legitimate business reasons.
