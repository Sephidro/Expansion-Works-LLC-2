05 · Common questions

Hard questions. Straight answers.

I've paid for a website before and nothing happened. Why would this be different?
Because most designers start with design and end with design. I start with research, who your buyer actually is, what language they use when they describe their problem, and what they need to believe before they'll commit. The design comes after the strategy. If your last site didn't work, it's because nobody did that work first.
How long does the build take?
Work starts within 48 hours of payment. A standard build is complete in 3 to 4 weeks. If your industry is one I've already researched, it goes faster. I'll tell you upfront which it is.
Do you write the copy or do I have to send you content?
I write it. That's the point. You tell me about your best clients, the ones who were easy to close and the problems they came with. I do the research to validate what their buyer actually sounds like online, and I write copy in their language. You review, we refine, it goes live. You don't write a word.
What if I'm not ready to commit to a retainer?
Start with the build at $2,500. You get a fully researched, conversion-built site with a lead magnet included. If it performs and you want the growth system added on, we pick up from there. No pressure to commit to more before you've seen what the foundation does.
What kind of businesses do you work with?
Service businesses where one new client is worth a lot, $10K or more. Consultants, specialized practitioners, agencies, B2B operators. The math has to make sense: if one client covers the cost of the build, we should talk. If your average sale is $400, I'm probably not the right fit, and I'll tell you that upfront.
