LEAD RECOVERY CASE // NYC CHARTER SCHOOL ENROLLMENT

207 people raised their hands. 105 finished after the follow-up system.

This school case shows how I found 207 unfinished applications, kept them visible, and helped 105 reach completion through reminders and human follow-up. A consulting firm has different buyers, sales cycles, and economics, so its results will vary.

207started, then abandoned
105later completed
~70remaining warm leads

THE FAILURE

207 families started the application and stopped before finishing.

Prospective families had already started an application. The unfinished step sat between “I am interested” and “I finished what the school needs next.”

Some families were delayed, confused, distracted, or waiting for a useful response. We kept every unfinished application on an active list until staff reached the family or recorded an outcome.

HOW IT WORKED

We kept every unfinished application visible and followed up.

Staff could see who had stopped, what they still needed, and who should contact them next.

ATTRIBUTION LIMITS

Limits of this case.

  • A consulting firm may see a different completion rate and economic result.
  • The school did not run a randomized experiment. Reminders, human follow-up, timing, school demand, and family circumstances can all affect completion.
  • Application completion and enrollment were tracked as separate outcomes.
  • The $1.7M figure is an associated-value estimate across two recorded years. Expansion Works did not collect that money, and the amount does not predict a future recovery.
  • A person still had to own the cases that automation could not resolve.
