THE PRE-SALE TRUST TEST

A prospect who has to chase you before paying assumes they will have to chase you after.

Your lead process is a sample of your delivery. In high-trust advisory work, silence, missing next steps, and proposals floating in an inbox do more than slow a deal. They create evidence that the engagement may feel the same way.

The output is not a stack. It is an order of operations.

Most software quizzes ask which features you want and route you to a vendor. StackBrief starts with real demand, follow-through, ownership, and where buyers stall. Software enters only after the first constraint is named.

KEEP

Protect what is still doing its job

  • Avoid migrations without evidence
  • Keep manual work while manual still wins
  • Do not punish the tools for a process gap

MEASURE

Choose the number that can prove the fix

  • Response time
  • Opportunities with a next action
  • Qualified conversations with an outcome

UPGRADE LATER

Know the trigger before adding complexity

  • More people need the same context
  • Manual follow-through begins failing
  • A proven workflow outgrows its owner

THE REFERRAL CAMOUFLAGE EFFECT

Referrals can make a broken process look healthy.

A warm introduction arrives with borrowed trust. It may wait, follow up twice, and fill in missing context. A buyer who found you through content, search, a partnership, or outbound will not. StackBrief tests whether the process can convert interest that does not arrive pre-sold.
