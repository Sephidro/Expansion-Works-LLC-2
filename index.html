WHAT YOUR FOLLOW-UP TELLS BUYERS

A prospect who has to chase you before paying assumes they will have to chase you after.

Your lead process is a sample of your delivery. In high-trust advisory work, silence, missing next steps, and proposals floating in an inbox make prospects picture the same delays after they hire you.

Fix the first place buyers stall. Buy software only when that fix needs it.

StackBrief starts with real demand, follow-up, ownership, and where buyers stall. It gives you the smallest useful fix, explains each choice, and tells you when an upgrade becomes worth it.

WHEN REFERRALS HIDE THE PROBLEM

Warm referrals may keep following up. Colder buyers usually will not.

A warm introduction arrives with borrowed trust. It may wait, follow up twice, and fill in missing context. A buyer who found you through content, search, a partnership, or outbound will not. StackBrief tests whether the process can convert interest that does not arrive pre-sold.
